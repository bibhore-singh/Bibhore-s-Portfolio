Why This Works Better

The Problem With Old Fact-Checkers & Our Smarter Solution

Most tools only look at one article in isolation. FAKEBUSTER focuses on who is publishing it and tracks their overall honesty over time.

⚠️

The Old Way: Checking One Article in Isolation

If an article is satire or has a joke headline, normal tools mistakenly flag it as fake. At the same time, fake news creators easily slip past filters by slightly rewording text.

🎯

Our Way: Tracking the Publisher's Honesty

By keeping track of multiple stories from the same author or website, we build an honest trust score. If a website repeatedly produces fake news, its posts are automatically downranked in social media feeds.
